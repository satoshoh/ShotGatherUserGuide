This Privacy Policy describes how shotgather.com (hereinafter, the "Provider") handles information in connection with the macOS application "ShotGather" (hereinafter, the "App") and the website shotgather.com (hereinafter, the "Website").

1. Data Handling by the App

The App operates locally on the user's Mac. The App does not transmit to, or collect on, external servers any of the following data:

  • Photo and video files, and their metadata (such as EXIF information)
  • Import destination folder paths
  • App settings and preferences

Path information for the import destination folder selected by the user is retained, by means of the macOS security mechanism (Security-Scoped Bookmark), only on the user's Mac device.

The macOS notification facility (UserNotifications) relies on a standard macOS mechanism provided by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies to such mechanism.

2. Error Reporting

To improve the quality and stability of the App, the Provider uses GlitchTip (glitchtip.com), a cloud-based error tracking service, to collect error information. The following data may be collected when an error occurs:

  • Crash logs and error messages
  • Stack traces
  • Device information (Mac model)
  • Operating system version
  • App version

Photos, videos, and personally identifiable information are not transmitted through this error reporting.

For details on how GlitchTip handles data, please refer to GlitchTip's Privacy Policy.

3. Website Analytics

The Website uses Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage statistics for the purpose of improving the Website. The following types of data may be collected:

  • Page views and navigation paths
  • Device and browser information
  • Referral sources

This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected through Google Analytics on the Website.

4. Cookies

The Website uses Cookiebot to manage cookie consent. The following types of cookies may be used:

  • Necessary cookies: Required for the cookie consent functionality to operate.
  • Analytics cookies: Used by Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage data (set only with user consent).

Users can manage their cookie preferences at any time through the Cookiebot consent banner displayed on the Website. For details, please refer to Cookiebot's Privacy Policy.

The App itself does not use cookies.

5. Third-Party Services

The App and the Website rely on the following third-party services. Each service is subject to its own terms and privacy policy.

  • Apple (macOS, App Store, UserNotifications, ImageIO): Apple Privacy Policy
  • GlitchTip (error tracking): GlitchTip Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics (website analytics): Google Privacy Policy
  • Cookiebot (cookie consent management): Cookiebot Privacy Policy

6. Children's Privacy

The App is offered on the App Store for users aged 13 and older. The Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact us (see Section 8) so that we can promptly delete such information.

7. Changes to This Policy

The Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App or the Website after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
